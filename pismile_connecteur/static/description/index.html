PI-SPI Payment Requests

Send your customer invoices as PI-SPI payment requests. Your customer approves in their bank or mobile money app, and Odoo records and reconciles the payment for you.

1. Invoice

Confirm a customer invoice in XOF as usual, then click Request PI-SPI payment (Demander le paiement PI-SPI).

2. Customer approves

The request reaches the customer's bank or mobile money app, showing your company name and the invoice number. One tap to pay.

3. Paid and reconciled

The money lands directly on your own account. Odoo creates the payment in your bank journal and reconciles it with the invoice.

Features

  • Request PI-SPI payment (Demander le paiement PI-SPI) button on confirmed customer invoices in XOF (CFA franc).
  • Full amount or a deposit, never more than the amount due; custom reason shown to the customer.
  • Payment created in the journal of your choice and reconciled with the invoice, with the PI-SPI end-to-end reference for bank reconciliation.
  • Instant updates through a signed webhook, or every 5 minutes without a webhook (Odoo behind a firewall is fine).
  • The customer's PI-SPI alias (phone number) is remembered on the contact.
  • PI-SPI Requests menu (Demandes PI-SPI): pending, paid, refused and expired requests, with filters.
  • New request in one click after a refusal or an expiry.
  • Test mode: simulated payments, no money moves.
  • Multi-company: one key and one webhook per company.

User interface in French, the language of the UEMOA markets this module is built for.

Requirements

This module connects Odoo to pismile, an external service that sends the payment requests over PI-SPI, the instant payment system of the BCEAO (Central Bank of West African States). You need:

  • Odoo 18 (Community or Enterprise) with Invoicing or Accounting, on your own server or Odoo.sh. Odoo Online does not accept third-party modules.
  • A pismile account (pismile.com) with the Collections module (Encaissement) and a subscription to the Odoo connector. Plans and prices are listed on pismile.com.
  • Your own PI-SPI access, provided by your bank or mobile money operator in the UEMOA. Money is paid straight to your account: pismile never holds your funds.
  • Invoices in XOF (CFA franc).

A free test key lets you try every case (paid, refused, expired) without real payments.

Data sent to pismile

Nothing is sent until a user clicks Request PI-SPI payment and confirms. For that request, Odoo sends to pismile: the invoice reference, the amount, the customer's PI-SPI alias and name, the due date, and the internal Odoo invoice and database identifiers (to match the answer). Odoo then reads the status of its own requests. No other data from your database is sent.

The pismile key is stored in your Odoo settings; webhooks are verified with an HMAC-SHA256 signature. pismile privacy policy: pismile.com/confidentialite

Installation

  1. Install the module, then open Invoicing / Accounting > Configuration > Settings, block pismile.
  2. Paste the key created in the pismile console (Encaissement > Connecteurs > Odoo) and choose the bank journal. Click Test connection (Tester la connexion).
  3. Optional: copy the webhook URL shown in the settings into the pismile console, then paste the signing secret back into Odoo.
  4. Confirm an invoice in XOF and click Request PI-SPI payment.

The step-by-step guide (in French) is in the pismile documentation: pismile.com/docs/connecteurs/odoo
